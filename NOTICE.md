# Umbra notices

Umbra is a native macOS reimplementation of the Moonlight Qt client.

The protocol implementation incorporates `moonlight-common-c` from the
Moonlight project. Moonlight Qt and moonlight-common-c are copyright their
respective contributors and are distributed under the GNU General Public
License, version 3. See `LICENSE` and the notices retained under
`Vendor/MoonlightCommon`.

The local `reference/moonlight-qt` checkout is excluded from this repository.
It exists only as an implementation and feature-parity reference.
